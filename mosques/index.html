Muslim Community Association

3003 Scott Blvd, Santa Clara, CA 95054, USA
The mosque complex is right besides my NVIDIA office. This is my daily prayer space for the summer of 2026. It has cafes, Islamic school, auditoriums and a bunch of facilities together. And you can stay throughout the day, which is a rare thing in US (actually even rare in BD). Very peaceful place amidst the chaos :)
